# Sources

Every important claim in this research pack is listed here with title, publisher, link,
publication date, and access date. Reliability is flagged. Conflicts are noted. All web
sources were accessed on **12 July 2026** unless stated otherwise.

Reliability key:
- **A -- Primary / authoritative:** the organisation that owns the fact (manufacturer,
  standards body, the app itself) or a peer-reviewed / official publication.
- **B -- Reliable secondary:** reputable body summarising primary evidence.
- **C -- Unverified in this session:** referenced in the design document or plausibly
  true, but not located or confirmed with a citable source here. Do not present as fact
  until verified.

## Profile rules

**S2. "High and low FODMAP foods"** -- Reliability A (the body that developed the diet)
Publisher: Monash University (Department of Gastroenterology / Monash FODMAP).
Accessed: 12 July 2026.
Link: https://www.monashfodmap.com/about-fodmap-and-ibs/high-and-low-fodmap-foods/

## Incumbent (Yuka) method

**S5. "How are food products rated?"** -- Reliability A (Yuka's own documentation)
Publisher: Yuka (Yuca SAS). Published: 13 October 2019; last updated: 29 June 2026.
Accessed: 12 July 2026.
Link: https://help.yuka.io/l/en/article/ijzgfvi1jq-how-are-food-products-scored
Supports (verbatim points): score = 60% nutritional quality (calculation based on
Nutri-Score), 30% additives (each additive rated risk-free/limited/moderate/high; a
single high-risk additive caps the product at 49/100), 10% organic bonus. This is also
the primary evidence that the Yuka score is a single fixed value computed the same way
for every user, with no input for an individual's dietary constraint.
Tone note: use this neutrally. It documents a design choice, not a failing.

## Conflicts and cautions

- **No direct source conflict** was found between S2 (Monash: wholemeal wheat bread is
  high-FODMAP) and S3 (SACN: increase fibre). They are not in conflict; they apply
  different criteria to the same food, which is precisely the point of the example.
- **Dose caveat (S2):** Monash's high/low labels are serving-dependent. State this.
- **Regulatory text (S4) and cognitive-load source (S9)** are cited from established
  knowledge but were not opened in this session. Verify both directly before publishing.
- **Interview consent (S13):** no direct-quote consent is recorded for any of the four
  participants. Publish paraphrase only, per `PUBLIC-INTERVIEW-SUMMARY.md`, until
  per-participant quote consent is confirmed and documented. Do not publish the raw
  transcript PDF or any detail beyond what that summary already anonymises.
